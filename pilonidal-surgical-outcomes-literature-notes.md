# Pilonidal Sinus Disease Surgical Outcomes — Literature Notes (First Pass)

**Review focus:** Long-term outcomes after surgery, prioritising **recurrence** and **postoperative infection**.
**Search:** PubMed database search (run now), filtered to roughly the last five years (2020–2025).
**Provenance:** All citations below come from this live search output; nothing is recalled from memory.

---

## Summary

The search returned **15 articles** — the set is **not thin**. Every record matched the query on surgical treatment of pilonidal sinus disease with outcomes (recurrence, wound infection, healing, etc.). One is a case report, one is a small pilot study, and one is a short Chinese-language clinical note, but all were returned by the search for surgical outcomes, so they are reported rather than omitted (and flagged in place).

## Citations — exactly as returned by the search

### 1. PMID 41278051
- **Title:** Evaluating Risk Factors for Surgical Site Occurrences: Infection and Wound Dehiscence Post Definitive Surgery for Sacrococcygeal Pilonidal Sinus Disease.
- **Journal:** Cureus, 2025
- **Takeaway:** Evaluates the rates of surgical site infection and wound dehiscence after definitive surgery for sacrococcygeal pilonidal sinus disease and identifies risk factors across multiple surgical techniques.

### 2. PMID 41074563
- **Title:** Sacrococcygeal Pilonidal Sinus Disease: A Decade-In-Review of Patient-Reported Outcome Measures.
- **Journal:** ANZ journal of surgery, 2026 (ahead-of-print)
- **Takeaway:** Reviews pilonidal sinus trends and how different surgical techniques map onto patient-reported outcome measures, noting that although recurrence and surgical site infection rates are well documented, technique-level effects on patient experience remain less characterised.

### 3. PMID 41018321
- **Title:** Negative Pressure Wound Therapy as an Alternative to Flap Reconstruction in an Extensive 16.5 × 11 cm Pilonidal Sinus Excision: A Case Report.
- **Journal:** Cureus, 2025
- **Takeaway:** (Case report — least central to the review.) Documents NPWT healing a 16.5 × 11 cm excision defect with sustained healing and no recurrence at up to two years, positioning NPWT as a less-invasive alternative to flap reconstruction for large excisional defects.

### 4. PMID 40956974
- **Title:** Comparing Square Excision and Transposition Flap Versus Modified Limberg Flap in Sacrococcygeal Pilonidal Sinus Disease Surgery: A New Technique With Fewer Wound Issues.
- **Journal:** Advances in skin & wound care, 2026 (ahead-of-print)
- **Takeaway:** Reports early outcomes of square excision with transposition flap versus the widely used modified Limberg flap, demonstrating a technique with fewer wound issues.

### 5. PMID 40522552
- **Title:** Evaluation of laser ablation for recurrent pilonidal sinus disease: treatment success, recurrence rates, and patient outcomes.
- **Journal:** Lasers in medical science, 2025
- **Takeaway:** Evaluates laser ablation specifically for recurrent pilonidal sinus disease, reporting treatment success, recurrence rates, complications and recovery outcomes.

### 6. PMID 40108765
- **Title:** A Comparitive Study Between Early Outcomes of Laser and Lay-Open Technique in Management of Simple Pilonidal Sinus.
- **Journal:** Lasers in surgery and medicine, 2025
- **Takeaway:** Compares early outcomes of laser versus lay-open technique for simple pilonidal sinus disease.

### 7. PMID 39982788
- **Title:** Outcome After Surgical Treatment for Chronic Pilonidal Sinus Disease: A Systematic Review of Common Surgical Techniques.
- **Journal:** Diseases of the colon and rectum, 2025
- **Takeaway:** Systematic review of surgical-treatment outcomes for chronic pilonidal sinus disease — the evidence synthesis most directly on-topic for the review's long-term-outcomes focus.

### 8. PMID 39893840
- **Title:** Laser Hair Removal May Be a Primary Treatment of Pilonidal Disease: A Pilot Study.
- **Journal:** Journal of pediatric surgery, 2025
- **Takeaway:** Pilot study of laser hair removal as a primary treatment for pilonidal disease; post-operative LHR has promise to reduce recurrence but is unproven as standalone primary therapy.

### 9. PMID 39800555
- **Title:** Limberg flap for previously recurrent complex sacrococcygeal pilonidal sinus surgeries.
- **Journal:** Acta chirurgiae plasticae, 2024
- **Takeaway:** Reports the Limberg flap's effects on wound infection rate, seroma formation, postoperative pain relief, recurrence rates, return to work, cosmesis and patient satisfaction in recurrent complex disease.

### 10. PMID 39699772
- **Title:** Sinus laser therapy versus Karydakis flap procedure in the management of pilonidal sinus disease: a comparative analysis of intraoperative parameters and postoperative outcome.
- **Journal:** Techniques in coloproctology, 2024
- **Takeaway:** Compares minimally invasive sinus laser therapy (SiLaT) against the Karydakis flap on intraoperative parameters and postoperative outcomes.

### 11. PMID 39313437
- **Title:** [Clinical application of parasacral artery perforator flap in the treatment of Pilonidal Sinus Diseases]. (Chinese-language note)
- **Journal:** Zhonghua wei chang wai ke za zhi (Chinese journal of gastrointestinal surgery), 2024
- **Takeaway:** (Least central — a short clinical note.) Report of parasacral artery perforator flap application in pilonidal sinus disease management.

### 12. PMID 39174833
- **Title:** Long-term follow-up of pilonidal sinus disease treated by radial laser surgery.
- **Journal:** Langenbeck's archives of surgery, 2024
- **Takeaway:** Directly addresses the review's core question: long-term follow-up (recurrence, healing, satisfaction) of radial laser surgery for pilonidal sinus disease.

### 13. PMID 39140695
- **Title:** Results of Laser-assisted Closure (SiLaC) Surgery in Pilonidal Sinus Disease: Factors Associated With Success.
- **Journal:** Surgical laparoscopy, endoscopy & percutaneous techniques, 2024
- **Takeaway:** Examines efficacy and safety of SiLaC in a tertiary care setting and identifies factors associated with failure/success.

### 14. PMID 39136828
- **Title:** Efficacy and safety of RD2 Ver.02, a whole blood clot therapy, coupled with a minimally invasive procedure in pilonidal sinus: a phase II study.
- **Journal:** Techniques in coloproctology, 2024
- **Takeaway:** Phase II study evaluating the novel autologous whole-blood-clot product RD2 Ver.02 combined with a minimally invasive procedure for pilonidal sinus.

### 15. PMID 39045854
- **Title:** Treatment options for patients with pilonidal sinus disease: PITSTOP, a mixed-methods evaluation.
- **Journal:** Health technology assessment (Winchester, England), 2024
- **Takeaway:** Mixed-methods evaluation of how treatment options (surgical practice varies widely) are used in real-world management of pilonidal sinus disease.
