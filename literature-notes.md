## Summary by Outcome Domain

**Recurrence (primary focus):**
- PMID 40522552 (laser ablation for recurrent PSD), PMID 39174833 (long-term follow-up of radial laser surgery), and PMID 39140695 (SiLaC success factors) are the most directly on-topic for long-term recurrence data.
- PMID 39800555 (Limberg flap) and PMID 39699772 (SiLaT vs. Karydakis flap) report recurrence rates alongside infection and pain outcomes.
- PMID 39045854 (PITSTOP mixed-methods evaluation) provides context on the heterogeneity of existing evidence.

**Postoperative infection:**
- PMID 39800555 explicitly reports wound infection rate and seroma formation for the Limberg flap.
- PMID 39699772 and PMID 39140695 cover postoperative outcomes following laser-based surgical approaches.
- PMID 41018321 (NPWT case report) addresses infection and inflammation management in large excisions.

**Modality trends observed in this search result set:**
1. Laser-based approaches (SiLaT, SiLaC, radial laser, lay-open comparison) dominate recent publications (2024–2025).
2. Flap reconstruction (Limberg, Karydakis, parasacral artery perforator) remains a core comparative arm, especially for recurrent and complex disease.
3. Long-term follow-up data are still relatively scarce, which itself is a finding worth flagging for the review's rationale.
