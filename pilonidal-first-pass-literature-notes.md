## Caveats & quality flags

1. The search returned **14 records** — a reasonable first-pass yield; nothing tangential or from recollection was added.
2. **PMIDs 41074563 and 39313437 carry 2026 dates as returned by the database** (ahead-of-print / early-view items) — verify final volume/issue on PubMed before formal citation.
3. **PMID 40956974 has no publication date available** in the record.
4. **PMIDs 41018321 and 39313437 are low-weight** (case report; short Chinese-language note) and should not anchor the formal reference list.
5. Several minimally invasive series report **short- to medium-term** recurrence data rather than ≥5-year follow-up; the long-term anchor for a ≥5-year synthesis remains **PMID 39982788** (systematic review) plus PMID 41278051 for SSI/wound-dehiscence risk factors.
6. **Suggested follow-up pass:** long-term cohorts with explicit ≥5-year recurrence and postoperative infection endpoints, and head-to-head comparisons of laser-assisted closure vs. off-midline flap reconstruction.
