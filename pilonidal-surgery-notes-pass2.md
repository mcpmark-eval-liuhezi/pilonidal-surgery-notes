## Citation list (exactly as returned by the search)

### 1. PMID 41278051
- **Title:** Evaluating Risk Factors for Surgical Site Occurrences: Infection and Wound Dehiscence Post Definitive Surgery for Sacrococcygeal Pilonidal Sinus Disease.
- **Journal:** Cureus | **Year:** 2025
- **Takeaway:** Reports rates of surgical site infections (SSIs) and wound dehiscence after definitive SPD surgery across multiple techniques and identifies associated risk factors — directly relevant to the infection endpoint of this review.

### 2. PMID 41074563
- **Title:** Sacrococcygeal Pilonidal Sinus Disease: A Decade-In-Review of Patient-Reported Outcome Measures.
- **Journal:** ANZ journal of surgery | **Year:** 2026
- **Takeaway:** A decade-in-review of PSD that frames recurrence and SSI rates as well documented while technique-specific patient-reported outcome measures remain less understood, useful for framing PROM endpoints alongside recurrence.

### 3. PMID 41018321
- **Title:** Negative Pressure Wound Therapy as an Alternative to Flap Reconstruction in an Extensive 16.5 × 11 cm Pilonidal Sinus Excision: A Case Report.
- **Journal:** Cureus | **Year:** 2025
- **Takeaway:** Documents NPWT achieving complete epithelialization and sustained healing with no recurrence at 2-year follow-up in a huge post-excisional defect, positioning NPWT as a less invasive alternative to flap reconstruction with a longer wound-management course.

### 4. PMID 40995583
- **Title:** Pilot Study: Application of Lateralized Z-plasty in Pilonidal Sinus Defect Closure.
- **Journal:** Plastic and reconstructive surgery. Global open | **Year:** 2025
- **Takeaway:** Evaluates lateralized Z-plasty (LZP) for reducing horizontal wound tension — a recognized driver of recurrence and complication rates in traditional Z-plasty approaches.

### 5. PMID 40522552
- **Title:** Evaluation of laser ablation for recurrent pilonidal sinus disease: treatment success, recurrence rates, and patient outcomes.
- **Journal:** Lasers in medical science | **Year:** 2025
- **Takeaway:** Quantifies treatment success, recurrence rates, complications, and recovery after laser ablation specifically in **recurrent** PSD — on-point for a recurrence-focused review.

### 6. PMID 40108765
- **Title:** A Comparitive Study Between Early Outcomes of Laser and Lay-Open Technique in Management of Simple Pilonidal Sinus.
- **Journal:** Lasers in surgery and medicine | **Year:** 2025
- **Takeaway:** Compares early outcomes of laser ablation versus lay-open technique for simple PSD, informing technique selection for low-complexity cases.

### 7. PMID 39982788
- **Title:** Outcome After Surgical Treatment for Chronic Pilonidal Sinus Disease: A Systematic Review of Common Surgical Techniques.
- **Journal:** Diseases of the colon and rectum | **Year:** 2025
- **Takeaway:** **Highest-priority anchor citation** — a systematic review of outcomes after surgical treatment for chronic PSD across common techniques; this is the long-term evidence base to build the review's recurrence/infection synthesis on.

### 8. PMID 39893840
- **Title:** Laser Hair Removal May Be a Primary Treatment of Pilonidal Disease: A Pilot Study.
- **Journal:** Journal of pediatric surgery | **Year:** 2025
- **Takeaway:** Investigates postoperative laser hair removal (LHR) as a primary recurrence-reduction strategy alongside surgical hocon萬, relevant to the adjunctive prevention of recurrence after surgery.

### 9. PMID 39699772
- **Title:** Sinus laser therapy versus Karydakis flap procedure in the management of pilonidal sinus disease: a comparative analysis of intraoperative parameters and postoperative outcome.
- **Journal:** Techniques in coloproctology | **Year:** 2024
- **Takeaway:** Head-to-head comparison of minimally invasive sinus laser therapy (SiLaT) vs. Karydakis flap on intraoperative parameters and postoperative outcomes, supporting the minimally-invasive vs. flap trade-off discussion.

### 10. PMID 39578271
- **Title:** Does the holmium laser have a place in the treatment of pilonidal cysts? (Pilolas study).
- **Journal:** Lasers in medical science | **Year:** 2024
- **Takeaway:** Retrospective cohort (102 patients) showing holmium laser achieved 90.7% healing at 1 month (SSI in 15.9%, recurrence in 17.2%, pain common and prolonged) and is not a meaningful alternative to SiLaT — useful negative evidence on cost/access trade-offs in minimally invasive options.

## Sequencing note for the review lead

- **Anchor citation for long-term synthesis:** PMID 39982788 (systematic review, *Diseases of the colon and rectum*, 2025) plus PMID 41278051 (Cureus, 2025) for SSI/wound-dehiscence risk factors.
- **Minimally invasive vs. flap evidence:** PMIDs 40522552, 40108765, 39699772, 39578271 cover laser ablation, lay-open, SiLaT, and Karydakis comparisons.
- **Honest limitation:** Several series report short- to medium-term recurrence; explicit ≥5-year recurrence and infection endpoints are still thin in this result set. A follow-up pass should target long-term cohorts with those endpoints.
