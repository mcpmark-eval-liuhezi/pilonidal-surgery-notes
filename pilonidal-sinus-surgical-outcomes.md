## Citation list (as returned by the search)

### A. Systematic reviews and decade-in-reviews

1. **PMID 39982788 — Outcome After Surgical Treatment for Chronic Pilonidal Sinus Disease: A Systematic Review of Common Surgical Techniques.** *Diseases of the colon and rectum*, 2025. **Takeaway:** The most useful synthesis for technique-by-technique presentation of outcomes — and a reminder that the choice of operation remains controversial — for building technique-by-technique comparisons in the review.

2. **PMID 41074563 — Sacrococcygeal Pilonidal Sinus Disease: A Decade-In-Review of Patient-Reported Outcome Measures.** *ANZ journal of surgery*, 2026. **Takeaway:** A decade-in-review of patient-reported outcome measures in PSD, noting that while recurrence and SSI rates are well documented, less is known about how different surgical techniques affect patient-reported outcomes.

### B. Infection and postoperative-wound complications

3. **PMID 41278051 — Evaluating Risk Factors for Surgical Site Occurrences: Infection and Wound Dehiscence Post Definitive Surgery for Sacrococcygeal Pilonidal Sinus Disease.** *Cureus*, 2025. **Takeaway:** This is the group's primary direct evidence on the secondary-outcome topic — rates of surgical site infection and wound dehiscence, and the factors associated with them — across multiple surgical techniques.

4. **PMID 40956974 — Comparing Square Excision and Transposition Flap Versus Modified Limberg Flap in Sacrococcygeal Pilonidal Sinus Disease Surgery: A New Technique With Fewer Wound Issues.** *Advances in skin & wound care*, publication year **not returned by the search**. **Takeaway:** Presents early outcomes of a square excision and transposition flap technique compared with the modified Limberg flap, reported as having fewer wound issues.

### C. Minimally invasive and laser techniques

5. **PMID 40108765 — A Comparitive Study Between Early Outcomes of Laser and Lay-Open Technique in Management of Simple Pilonidal Sinus.** *Lasers in surgery and medicine*, 2025. **Takeaway:** A comparative study of early outcomes of laser versus lay-open technique in simple PSD.

6. **PMID 40522552 — Evaluation of laser ablation for recurrent pilonidal sinus disease: treatment success, recurrence rates, and patient outcomes.** *Lasers in medical science*, 2025. **Takeaway:** Evaluates the efficacy of laser ablation in patients with recurrent PSD, covering treatment success, recurrence rates, complications, and recovery outcomes.

7. **PMID 39699772 — Sinus laser therapy versus Karydakis flap procedure in the management of pilonidal sinus disease: a comparative analysis of intraoperative parameters and postoperative outcome.** *Techniques in coloproctology*, 2024. **Takeaway:** Compares sinus laser therapy (SiLaT) with the Karydakis flap surgery on intraoperative parameters and postoperative outcomes.

8. **PMID 39893840 — Laser Hair Removal May Be a Primary Treatment of Pilonidal Disease: A Pilot Study.** *Journal of pediatric surgery*, 2025. **Takeaway:** A pilot study exploring laser hair removal as a primary treatment strategy for pilonidal disease, beyond its established post-operative role in reducing recurrence.

### D. Flap and closure-technique evidence

9. **PMID 39800555 — Limberg flap for previously recurrent complex sacrococcygeal pilonidal sinus surgeries.** *Acta chirurgiae plasticae*, 2024. **Takeaway:** Reports Limberg flap outcomes for previously recurrent complex PSD, covering wound infection rate, seroma formation, postoperative pain relief, recurrence, return to work, and patient satisfaction.

10. **PMID 41018321 — Negative Pressure Wound Therapy as an Alternative to Flap Reconstruction in an Extensive 16.5 × 11 cm Pilonidal Sinus Excision: A Case Report.** *Cureus*, 2025. **Takeaway:** A retrospective single-centre cohort (69 patients, ages ≤18, Feb 2023–Aug 2024) evaluating a structured tiered outpatient pathway — recurrence occurred in 26.1% of the 23 patients who needed operative intervention but only 2.2% of the 46 managed conservatively (Fisher's exact p = 0.0045).

## Notes for the group

- **Core secondary-outcome source:** PMID 41278051 is the group's primary source on postoperative infection and wound dehiscence rates plus associated risk factors.
- **Minimally invasive bias:** several records (PMIDs 41074563, 40108765, 40522552, 39699772, 39893840, 41018321) lean toward patient-reported outcome measures, laser, or wound-management approaches; these should be weighed against the flap-technique evidence (PMIDs 40956974, 39699772, 39800555) in the discussion.
- **Follow-up caveat:** most records in this pass report follow-up of one year or two years (e.g., the two-year recurrence-free follow-up for the NPWT case report); longer, recurrence-focused horizons may warrant a second search pass covering studies with ≥5-year follow-up.
- **Date range:** 2024–2026 with heavy 2026 weighting, reflecting the recency of the underlying search data; no pre-2020 items were returned and none have been included.
